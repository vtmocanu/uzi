## Iteration-limit hold (#3738)

- Both harnesses may park completed per-invocation iteration exhaustion as `paused` / `iteration_budget_exhausted` for exactly `issue`, `mr_rework`, `ci_fix`, `task`, `prompt`, `self_improve`: non-interactive, no review target or isolated fetch, exact persisted zero completion attempts, negotiated `iteration_park_version: 1`. Absent/unknown support keeps legacy failures; old workers ignore the additive field. Prior-attempt dispositions, idle and #3139 stay unchanged. Explicit owner Resume grants a fresh finite invocation allowance, not a lifetime reset or wall extension; repeated exhaustion needs Resume again. Spent wall requires Extend then Resume; Extend alone does not resume. Explicit owner set-token-and-resume uses the same wall guard; availability alone never resumes. Preserve resolvable worker/session affinity, compatible session resume or existing recovery disclosure. Bounded best-effort capture preserves clone/HOME/session/custody; failed publication or degraded capture may still park, but only a matching ACK confirms pause. Uncertain delivery stops with local retention and no server-paused claim. The hold has no expiry and holds lane/worker; local retention is not durable backup and grants no Stop or publication authority. See [iteration-limit instructions](../docs/run-pause.md#paused-at-the-iteration-limit) and [ADR-1225](../adr/1225-structural-completion-interlock-invariants.md). (AI-synced 2026-11-01)

## Codex bounded Read (#296)

- Text Read accepts path/file_path and optional one-based positive safe integer offset (default 1), limit 1–2000 (default 200); forward excerpts preserve UTF-8/BOM and LF/CRLF, cap at 64 KiB, and report size/content/offset/linesReturned/partialLastLine/truncated without duplicate base64 or a cursor. Strict helper validation retains the ordinary read's 1 MiB ceiling and neutral errors. Ordinary read runs first; success retains whole-body binary detection: invalid UTF-8 or NUL returns exact base64 only without explicit ranges or tail, and ranged/tail binary is refused. The ceiling exceptions are one read_range fallback after an explicit range or one read_tail fallback after an explicit tail, each requiring helper-origin E_OVERSIZE, with provenance minted by the client for a correlated helper error, never trusted from wire origin or attached to local errors. Forward fallback uses an independently confined second open and its coherent data/metadata, bounds scanning by its opening size without a snapshot guarantee, caps actual reads including lookahead at 64 MiB in chunks up to 32 KiB (up to 68,157,441 bytes combined with a growing first read), and validates UTF-8/NUL only through the deterministic requested prefix frontier; unread tail is ignored. Incomplete fallback results alone add incomplete/reason=scan_budget/scannedBytes; cross-check activity records limit rather than successful read evidence. The fallback helper's five-second deadline is cooperative; each sequential client request has a 30-second allowance (up to 60 seconds plus overhead), without an absolute callback/storage completion guarantee. Root and child replies must fit the existing 4 MiB transport cap. Optional text-only tail is an integer 1–2000, exclusive with the presence of offset or limit; invalid combinations fail as bad_args before I/O. Successful ordinary tail reads validate the whole body, including bytes before the suffix. Tail returns the most recent whole lines within 64 KiB, preserving LF/CRLF without a phantom terminal line and retaining the file-leading BOM when selection starts at zero; an oversized final line returns a UTF-8 boundary suffix with partialFirstLine=true and linesReturned=1. Tail fallback independently opens a confined descriptor pinned to its opening size, ignores growth and treats any short read/shrink as E_IO. Its shared rangeScanBytes budget charges actual reads including prefetch/lookbehind in chunks up to 32 KiB, with fixed output-plus-chunk-plus-three-byte boundary memory. Its deterministic text frontier is the returned suffix, preceding LF, and at most three lookbehind bytes needed at the RAW size-minus-64-KiB boundary to validate a crossing scalar before trimming; surplus prefetch and other bytes outside the frontier are not validated. Invalid UTF-8/NUL within it is E_NOT_TEXT/public bad_args. Output-cap stops are complete; budget exhaustion returns proven whole lines with incomplete/reason=scan_budget/scannedBytes. Tail public output is size/content/tail/fromEnd/linesReturned/partialFirstLine/truncated, with truncated exactly when preceding bytes exist; completed results omit incomplete/reason/scannedBytes, and tail results carry no absolute line numbers, offset, cursor or duplicate base64. The broker independently validates tail responses; completed tail cross-check activity is ok without line coordinates, incomplete is limit without successful read evidence. The same cooperative helper/client deadlines and absence of snapshot or absolute completion guarantees apply. Full contract: [ADR 0296](../adr/0296-codex-bounded-text-reads.md) and its forward/tail fallback extension [ADR 3073](../adr/3073-bounded-ranged-reads.md). (AI-synced 2026-10-30)

## Feature #19 — Admin settings & autopilot label

Tracked as GitLab issue vtmocanu/uzi#19; PRD at `prds/done/19-admin-settings-and-autopilot.md`.

- Autopilot: adding the autopilot label (alongside the run-eligibility label, default `uzi`) to an issue in GitLab normally runs it end to end without uzi interaction. With Plan cross-check enabled, a Claude lead proceeds only after Codex approves, and a Codex lead only after a read-only Claude checker approves, the latest exact plan and the handoff is acknowledged; the checker is always the opposite family. Eligible REVISE automatically returns fenced untrusted checker advice to a round-capable lead (a Codex lead without spending its human revision budget) within the server-counted budget; exhaustion, BLOCK, timeout and other check failures retain their human fallback. A Codex lead on a worker without `cross_check_codex_lead_v1` keeps the older park to a human gate. Irrecoverable preparation receipts or human-presentation ACK loss fails terminally; unresolved preparation ACKs after three attempts and an acknowledged forced human gate also fail. Historical findings do not certify a human revision. See [Cross-check](../docs/cross-check.md). (AI-synced 2026-10-08)
- Automatic Plan cross-check revisions use `PLAN_CROSS_CHECK_MAX_REVISIONS` (default 4, at most five checked candidates, integer 0–4), not human `runs.revise_count` or `PLAN_MAX_REVISIONS`. Superseded attempts consume the global limit + 1 candidate budget across claim generations. First-candidate enabled/limit snapshots are immutable and copied to subsequent rounds; configuration changes affect new checks only. `cross_check_rounds_v1` gates automatic leads and later-round children; older workers retain the REVISE human gate, while capable zero-budget workers park as exhausted. Each candidate gets a fresh deadline. Before an automatic revision, validated preparation/usage/reconciliation must release the actual reservation successfully, then release and await the checked-state barrier successfully; this path creates no human presentation or `awaiting_approval` report. Human inputs cannot supply automatic provenance. Explicit choices remain authoritative: older `.env` files explicitly setting 2 keep 2 after upgrading until the operator removes the line or sets 4; only installations without the line take the new default. (AI-synced 2026-10-17)
