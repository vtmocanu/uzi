3 · The thing that blocks section 2

visible in the strip above, not just in the cost

Estimated tokens out is lower than confirmed tokens out

Look at the second stat in section 2: ~18.1k estimated across the whole run against 41.8k confirmed for the Plan phase alone. A live figure that covers more work cannot legitimately be smaller. Per-call output_tokens reads p50 3, max 73 — a message_start snapshot, not a final count.

This is why M0 gates the PRD, and it is worse than a pricing error: it makes the panel self-contradictory in a way a user can see without knowing anything about our data paths. Ship section 2 with this unresolved and the first question is not "is the estimate accurate", it is "why does your own panel disagree with itself".

And it understates cost by roughly a third

On the Plan phase, where both numbers exist: deduped estimate $2.37 against billed $3.53, −33%. Feed the same estimator a real output count and it lands at $3.40, −3.9%. The missing output is worth 29% of the phase.

Nothing else here is in doubt
