3. Default jobs: On-deck sweep

On-deck sweep

new

Drains a triaged backlog a little at a time. Every 10 minutes it sends the oldest on-deck issue to uzi while you have room, then removes the label.

label on-deck + uzi · every 10 min
1 at a time · limit 4, room 2

Planned-work sweep

Daily: starts up to 3 issues labelled Planned that also carry uzi.

label Planned + uzi · daily 02:00 UTC
3 at a time · no limit

Bug triage

Daily: works up to 4 bug issues that also carry uzi.

label bug + uzi · daily 00:00 UTC
4 at a time · no limit

"When to send issues" is a setting on every label sweep, so it can be added to any sweep, default or custom.
