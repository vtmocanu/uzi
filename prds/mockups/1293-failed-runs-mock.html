What the number means

  • Fail rate = failed ÷ finished, where finished = completed + cancelled + plan rejected + failed. Active runs never enter the denominator.
  • Plan rejected is your decision, not a factory failure. It stays in the denominator but out of the numerator, and gets its own hatched segment.
  • chat and judge runs are excluded, matching what the Runs page shows.
  • Two windows, all-time and last 7 days, same as the usage figures beside it.
  • Top causes come from fail_origin, counted over the same scope and window.

What changes to ship it

  • UsageDTO gains finished_runs, failed_runs, cancelled_runs, rejected_runs and a small fail_origins map. No migration.
  • Three queries in runtime.sql grow the counts: SelfUsage, AdminUsageTotals, AdminUsagePerUser.
  • Web: the block inside UsageCards.tsx plus two table columns. CLI: uzi admin usage gets the same two columns.
  • Variant A adds nothing to the page's height on a fresh install: the block hides until the scope has one finished run.
