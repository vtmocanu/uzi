PRD #2278: Docker-capable ephemeral workers

Current (main)

Hosted workers

2 of 2 used — delete one to provision another

A hosted worker runs in the cluster, not on your machine: there is no join token to copy and no container to start. It shows up under Your workers and comes online on its own, and you delete it there like any other worker. Tick Docker-capable to give its agent a rootless, isolated Docker daemon (for docker and docker compose); it costs extra CPU and storage and needs an instance that offers the docker tier.

When on, uzi spins up a throwaway hosted worker on demand when one of your runs needs a capability no online worker has. After the run finishes, the worker may be kept warm, by default for up to 2 hours unless your admin turned that off, so a follow-up run on the same repository and branch can reuse it; then it is removed. While kept warm it still counts toward your ephemeral worker limit. This is experimental, and each new ephemeral worker pays a one-time ~2.6 GiB tool-cache cold start.

Persistent worker
2 of 2 used — delete one to provision another

Runs in the cluster, not on your machine: no join token, no container to start. It shows up under Your workers and you delete it there. Docker-capable gives it a Docker daemon for container builds and tests, at extra CPU and storage.

Ephemeral workers

uzi spins up a throwaway hosted worker when a run needs a capability no online worker has, or when every capable worker stays busy. It may be kept warm for up to 2 hours for a follow-up run on the same branch, and counts toward your ephemeral limit meanwhile. Docker-capable includes Docker for repositories your admin allows, at extra CPU and storage.

Agreed UX: the two ephemeral controls share ONE row (no nested, indented checkbox); ONE short paragraph covers both; no "experimental" label and no cold-start size; no "rootless" promise in either section (rootless:false is a supported posture). Docker-capable is hidden when the instance offers no docker tier, saves on change, keeps its value while auto-provision is off.
