Sidebar icon motion

Mock for issue #4510. Hover, focus or click any row: its icon plays one short animation tied to what the page does, then rests at the unchanged glyph. The setting is per user in Settings > Appearance and off by default. The active-row highlight and the badges work exactly as today.

Rules (see #4510 for the full contract)

  • One-shot. Each hover, focus-visible or click plays once and rests. A new gesture mid-play restarts it; nothing queues, loops or moves on its own.
  • 0.7s to 1.8s per icon, all parts included. The ~60ms press scale on click is exempt.
  • Only the SVG moves. The icon wrapper stays put, so the collapsed-rail badge and severity dots never move.
  • Off by default, scoped by one root attribute. With the setting off no animation rule matches. Turning it off cancels a play in progress.
  • Reduced motion wins. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing animates, even with the setting on.
  • CSS only. Keyframes on named SVG sub-parts plus a class toggled from the row's handlers; no animation library.
