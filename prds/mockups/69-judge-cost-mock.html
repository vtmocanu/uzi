Judge run: time · tokens · cost on the review panel

Today a judge run records its cost nowhere and shows nothing but via opus. This mocks the four ways the captured time / token / cost could surface on the reviewed run's Run review card. Same card, same tokens as the app — only the cost surface differs.

Chosen: Option C (the 4-tile strip). The other three are kept here as the design-decision record.

Numbers are illustrative: an opus judge over one compacted trace ≈ 38k in / 1.9k out, ~$0.71, ~9s. Cost also folds silently into your usage totals regardless of which surface you pick.
