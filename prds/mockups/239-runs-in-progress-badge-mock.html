What the number means

The count of your runs that are still working — anything not completed, failed, or cancelled. So queued, running, awaiting_approval, awaiting_input and limit_wait all count.

Which of those to include is the one design call worth settling in the PRD — e.g. whether a run parked on your approval should read as "in progress" or be split out. This mock counts every non-terminal run.

It sits beside the badges you already have

  • 3 Notifications — unread
  • 5 Runs — in progressNEW
  • 4 Judge — to triage
  • 2 Workers — need attention (red = go look now)

Runs reuses the brand "count" tone (a queue to get to), not the red "alert" tone the Workers badge uses for something that needs you right now.

Collapse the rail, keep the signal

On the icon rail there is no room for a number, so the badge becomes a brand dot on the icon — the same fallback Notifications, Judge and Workers already use. Screen readers still hear "5 in progress".
