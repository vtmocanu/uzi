### Added

- **Admins can issue a one-time account reset link for a user who forgot their password (#3989).**
  Redeeming it sets a new password, ends the user's browser sessions and deletes their model credentials, Codex logins and vault; the account is left protected and the next password login creates a fresh vault. The seed admin and the issuing admin cannot be reset by link, and SSO-only users are not yet eligible, and runs already executing keep their credential.
