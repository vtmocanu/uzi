- **Worker startup records the outcome of every `.retire-*` retirement scratch parent its bounded root listing reaches (#4143).**
  Observation only: one total line per startup whenever the runner root exists (none when it is missing); each parent is kept and nothing is deleted, chowned or moved; bytes are measured read-only within one 30 s startup budget. Disable with chart `workers.epochReclaim.retireScratchOutcomes: false`; reclaim stays open pending a durable parent binding.
