### Fixed

- **Enabling the extra CA no longer rolls a uid-split worker whose Secret lacks the Codex CA key (#4702).**
  The controller renders `UZI_CODEX_CA_FILE` only after confirming the worker's Secret holds the key; on upgrade a worker whose template already carries it drifts and, once its roll is admitted, drops it, and workers created with the extra CA before this release have no recorded evidence and lose the Codex CA until reprovisioned. The evidence survives a controller restart during a /data recycle that keeps the Deployment.
