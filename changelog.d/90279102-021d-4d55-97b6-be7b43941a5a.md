### Changed

- **Per-run cache caps stop at 15 GiB on large worker volumes (#4576).**
  `UZI_RUN_CACHE_CAP_MAX_BYTES` (default 15 GiB, `off` disables it) now bounds the soft cache cap, so a 100 GiB volume caps at 15 GiB instead of 25 GiB, at the cost of more Go cache rebuilds and uncounted preventive parks; it is a mitigation, not a within-turn bound.
