### Added

- **Admin health shows a dry run of hibernated-worker volume retirement (#4208).**
  A new instance-scoped `fleet.hibernation_retirement` check lists which long-hibernated hosted workers a later release could retire and which are blocked and why, from an inventory the worker reports before it hibernates on the Docker lane; nothing is deleted and no setting enables deletion, and a worker with nothing blocking it is only ever "would retire only after M2 verifies" the actual PV reclaim policy and pod exclusion.
