### Fixed

- **Mid-turn checkpoint publishes no longer die at the 15 s listener timeout (#3847).**
  `POST /api/worker/runs/{id}/publish` now sets its own deadlines (60 s upload read, 455 s write) and an upload timeout answers 408 instead of 400; a cap of 4 concurrent publishes process-wide and 2 per worker answers 429 "busy" with `Retry-After: 5`, which the worker retries, and a worker naming a run it does not hold gets 404 before taking a slot. The worker gives checkpoint publish a separate 480 s allowance shared across its retries (`WORKER_HTTP_TIMEOUT` is unchanged), and an HTTP/2 stream reset is now reported as cause `stream_reset` rather than `unknown`.
