### Fixed

- **Memory-pressure parks settle their source use, so a fresh worker continues instead of failing (#3638).**
  A parked flight now ends its source use only on a witnessed park and a durable end, recorded in a versioned `memoryPark` journal field; with no such proof the fresh worker re-parks at the same hold (Resume re-parks until #3639), and workers built before this change reject the field and fail closed on it.
