### Added

- **Admins can hibernate and wake idle hosted workers (#4205).**
  Hibernating scales the worker's Deployment to 0 and keeps its row, join-token Secret and volumes; it is off by default behind `worker_hibernation_enabled`, wake is manual (or disabling the setting wakes all), and downgrading needs the setting off and migrations 00428 then 00427 rolled back before an older api.
