### Added

- **Hosted Docker workers contain a pod-epoch attempt-tree deletion phase that ships switched off (#3431).**
  The `pod_epoch_reclaim` log gains `deleted`, `deleted_bytes`, `settled`, `kept_*` and `reclaim_bytes_state` fields and the `reclaim_complete` and `reclaim_partial` reasons, but `EPOCH_RECLAIM_DELETION_ENABLED` is `false`, so production deletes nothing until a separate owner decision; the worker also advertises `recovery_run_open_holds_v1` for the run-wide open-hold count the phase requires.
