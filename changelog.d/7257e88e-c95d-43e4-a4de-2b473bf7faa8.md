### Changed

- **Verified pod epochs now reclaim bound `.retire-*` scratch parents on uid-split Docker workers (#4263).**
  Retirements record a durable `uzi-retire-scratch.<uuid>.binding` in the bare config; only `owner_terminal` and `verified_successor` parents that settle are emptied by the strict helper as the runner uid, with no chmod or chown, then removed by the worker. Partially removed (`removing`), unbound and other-class parents stay kept, and single-uid pods are unchanged. Disable with `workers.epochReclaim.retireScratchReclaim: false` (worker `UZI_RETIRE_SCRATCH_RECLAIM=0`; applies at the next worker startup); hosted rollout observation stays on #4143.
