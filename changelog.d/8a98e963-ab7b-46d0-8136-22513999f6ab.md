### Fixed

- **The history retention pass now keeps a recoverable backlog draining after statement timeouts instead of oscillating (#4788).**
  After a timeout a table remembers the batch size that timed out and keeps deleting just below it, so a table that times out at a larger batch but succeeds at a smaller one keeps draining. It logs a warning when it can only delete full batches below the maximum, which can mean the table is falling behind. A backlog the database cannot delete at any batch size is not drained by this change.
