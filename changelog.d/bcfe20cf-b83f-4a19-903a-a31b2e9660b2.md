### Changed

- **The pod-epoch reclaim pass carries a dark, default-off runner scratch phase (#4043).**
  It stays behind its own `EPOCH_SCRATCH_DELETION_ENABLED = false` gate, so nothing is censused or deleted and behavior is unchanged; the `pod_epoch_reclaim` log line gains a `scratch` object reading `state: "not_run"`, and any later byte figures are allocated-block estimates, not proof of freed storage.
