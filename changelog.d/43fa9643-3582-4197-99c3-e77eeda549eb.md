### Added

- **`uzi settings set` and `uzi settings reset` change your model and effort defaults from the CLI (#3264).**
  Covers worker model, effort, judge model, summary model (each with `--harness claude|codex`) and judge-runs-on over `PUT /api/me/model-settings`, which accepts a CLI token; `reset` follows the upstream default, `effort runtime` is the distinct Runtime default, and the generic settings write and consent switches stay cookie-only.
